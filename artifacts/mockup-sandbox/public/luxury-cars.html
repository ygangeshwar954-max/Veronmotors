What financing options are available for Veyron vehicles?
+
We offer bespoke financing solutions tailored to your portfolio, including balloon financing, lease programs, and direct purchase arrangements through our partner banks globally.
How does the bespoke customization process work?
+
Our atelier team works directly with you across 4–6 design sessions to configure your vehicle's exterior, interior, and mechanical specifications. Lead time is typically 14–20 weeks from final sign-off.
Do you offer white-glove delivery worldwide?
+
Yes. Every Veyron vehicle is delivered by our concierge logistics team with full insurance, enclosed transport, and a personal handover experience at your chosen location anywhere in the world.
What warranty and maintenance programs are included?
+
All vehicles include a 5-year unlimited-kilometre factory warranty, complimentary annual servicing for 3 years, and 24/7 roadside concierge across 40+ countries through our global partner network.

Absolutely. Our private experience days are held at curated track venues and scenic routes. Register your interest through the form above and a specialist will arrange the perfect event for you.
